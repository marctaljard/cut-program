01 Split

Rolling 6-session block. No reset day — day 7 is just the next session.

Push A → Pull A → Legs A → Push B → Pull B → Legs B → repeat
  • Each muscle: ~2.3× / week — above the 2× threshold, which is all the data supports
  • Per session: 5–8 sets per muscle, well under the ~11 ceiling
  • Per week: 13–18 direct sets per muscle
  • Six exercises a session, every session

02 Sessions

RIR = reps left in the tank. Everything sits in 6–12 now. Effort-matched, anything from 5 to 30 reps grows the same muscle, so the range is a practicality call, not a growth one. What is not flexible is proximity to failure — if a heavier lateral raise means swinging it up, you have traded real stimulus for a bigger number. Pick the load that lets you reach 0–1 RIR clean. Rest 2.5–3 min on the first two exercises, 90 sec after. Warm up the first lift with 2–3 ramping sets to your working weight; accessories need none. Type your working weight and last reps into each row — they save automatically.

03 Progression

  1. Pick a load you can hit at the bottom of the rep range at target RIR
  2. Add reps weekly until you reach the top of the range on every set
  3. Only then add load (+2.5 kg upper, +5 kg lower) and drop back to the bottom
  4. Reps before load, always. Chasing weight is how the rep range quietly becomes a triple

In a deficit, holding a lift is a win.

Expect machines and accessories to climb and the big compounds to hold. That’s success, not failure.

This is tuned for size, not maximal strength. Squat and bench appear about 1.2 times a week, which is plenty for hypertrophy but low for strength, where the lift is partly a skill needing 2–3 exposures. That trade is deliberate. Your 1RMs will lag what your muscle mass suggests — if you ever want the numbers, spend 4–6 weeks in the 4–6 rep range after the cut and they will catch up quickly.

Both hard sessions sit the day before an upper day, so running and intervals never precede legs. Zone 2 is the only one landing before a leg day. Lift first if it’s the same day.

The run — alternate each block

  • A: steady 5k, controlled. Don’t race it.
  • B: 5 × 1 km at goal pace, 90 sec walk. Or 6 × 800 m.

Bike intervals — Norwegian 4 × 4

10 min warm-up · 4 × 4 min at 90–95% max HR, 3 min easy between · 5 min cool-down.

The 4-minute length is the mechanism — long enough to hold you near VO2max. Don’t shorten it.

Zone 2 — 45–50 min

Cross trainer, incline treadmill walk (12–15%, 5–6 km/h), or bike. Conversational — full sentences, no gasping.
